### Zoho Junior Data Analyst - Interview Preparation Sheet

#### 1. Technical Questions:
- **Q1: Explain how you would optimize a slow-running SQL query joining 5 tables with millions of records.**
  *Answer Tip:* Mention EXPLAIN plans, creating targeted indexes, avoiding SELECT *, and leveraging CTEs or temporary tables.
- **Q2: Walk me through a data cleaning challenge you encountered in your projects.**
  *Answer Tip (STAR framework):* Cite your E-commerce project [Resume: Section 'Academic Projects'] where you handled null values and inconsistent dates using Pandas.

#### 2. Company & Behavioral Questions:
- **Q3: Why Zoho rather than a VC-funded startup or traditional IT consultancy?**
  *Answer Tip:* Highlight Zoho's bootstrapped independence, long-term product vision, and Chennai engineering heritage.
